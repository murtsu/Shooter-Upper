# Store page draft

**Title:** Shooter Upper

**Short description:** Fast arcade defense shooter. 16-bit looks, 8-bit chip sound, no filler.

**Long description:**
Fly low over a planet that wraps around you and keep your humanoids alive.
Landers snatch them, mutants hunt you, bombers seed the sky with mines, and
if you take too long the baiters come for you. Shoot the lander, catch the
falling humanoid, set it down safely. Lose them all and the planet blows up.

- Eight-screen scrolling world with radar
- Six enemy types, smart bombs, hyperspace gamble
- Keyboard and controllers, hot-plug, full rebinding
- Classic or modern steering
- Four grades from Easy to Insane
- Senseability: the game watches its own frame rate and your play, and tunes itself a little
- Amiga-style chrome fonts, copper bars and a wobbling logo
- Title song you can replace with your own MP3
- Optional CRT effect, integer scaling, fullscreen
- Every sound made by a software sound chip, every sprite drawn by code
- Local high score table
