# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Defender-style arcade shooter: wrapping planet, radar, landers, mutants,
  humanoid rescue, smart bombs, hyperspace.
- 16-bit look drawn by code, 8-bit chip sound synthesized by code.
- Amiga style chrome font, copper bars, sine scroller.
- Four grades: Easy, Normal, Hard, Insane.
- Senseability: watches frame rate and button presses, nudges enemy speed by
  at most 8 percent.
- Built-in 35 second chiptune title loop. Players can make their own song with
  the prompts in `docs/MUSIC.md` and save it as `theme.mp3` in the config
  folder.
- Idle parade of the whole cast after 20 seconds on the title screen.
- Error dialogs with suggestions, and a log file.
- Keyboard and SDL game controllers, hot-plugging, rebindable keys.
- Packages: Arch PKGBUILD, Ubuntu deb (24.04 or newer, amd64).
